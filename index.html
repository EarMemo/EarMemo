What EarMemo is for

耳记是给谁用的

EarMemo is for people who already have the long-form audio they want to listen to — podcast episodes downloaded as files, audiobooks purchased outside subscription stores, lecture recordings, conference talks, voice memos — and want a single focused tool to play them.

It is not a music player and not a streaming catalog. It is a player for content you control, optimized for listening sessions that last more than three minutes.

耳记是给那些已经有自己想听的长音频文件的人——下载好的播客 episode、订阅店以外买的有声书、讲座录音、会议发言、对话录音——想要一个专心的工具把它们播好。

它不是音乐播放器，不是流媒体目录。它是一个为"你自己拥有的内容"设计的播放器，专门针对超过三分钟的收听场景。

What stays on your device

Everything you do in EarMemo — imported audio, playback progress, listening stats, notes, bookmarks, and settings — stays on your device. If you have iCloud Backup enabled in iOS Settings, this data is included in your own iCloud backup as part of the standard system backup. EarMemo itself never initiates any sync or reads or writes any remote service.

What EarMemo does not do

  • Makes no network requests.
  • Includes no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • Requests no access to your contacts, location, photos, microphone, or notifications.
  • Has no account system; no sign-in is required.

System APIs used

EarMemo uses only standard local iOS APIs: UserDefaults (for settings and listening data), file modification timestamps (for sorting by date), background audio playback, and Live Activities (for the sleep-timer display). None of these APIs involve a network connection or any third party.

Children's privacy

EarMemo is rated 4+ and is safe for children. The zero-collection policy applies to every user regardless of age.

FAQ

What is EarMemo?

EarMemo is a local-first audio player for iOS, designed for long-form listening: podcasts, audiobooks, lectures, and voice recordings. All of your data — settings, playback progress, bookmarks, notes, and listening statistics — stays on your device. EarMemo does not connect to any server and does not require an account.

耳记是什么？

耳记（EarMemo）是一款 iOS 本地音频播放器，专为播客、有声书、公开课、讲座与对话录音而设计。你所有的数据——设置、播放进度、书签、笔记、收听统计——都只保存在你的设备上。耳记不连接任何服务器，也不需要账号。

How is EarMemo different from Apple Podcasts or Spotify?

EarMemo plays local audio files you already own — podcasts you've downloaded, audiobooks, lecture recordings, voice memos. It does not stream from a catalog, does not have a subscription, and does not require an account. It is designed for people who want one focused tool for long-form listening from files they control.

耳记和 Apple Podcasts、Spotify 有什么不同？

耳记播放的是你已经拥有的本地音频文件——你下载好的播客、有声书、讲座录音、对话录音。它不从任何目录流播放，没有订阅，也不需要账号。它是为那些"想要一个专心的工具去听自己已有的长内容"的人设计的。

Does EarMemo work offline?

Yes. EarMemo is local-first: it never connects to any server. All playback, statistics, and notes work without an internet connection. The app functions identically in airplane mode.

耳记可以离线使用吗？

可以。耳记是 local-first 的——从不连接任何服务器。播放、统计、笔记都不依赖网络。飞行模式下打开 App，所有功能完全正常。

Which audio and video formats does EarMemo support?

EarMemo supports common audio formats including mp3, m4a, aac, flac, and wav. Video files (mp4, mov, m4v) are played audio-only, so you can listen to the soundtrack of a video without watching it.

耳记支持哪些音视频格式？

耳记支持常见的音频格式：mp3、m4a、aac、flac、wav。视频文件（mp4、mov、m4v）会按音频播放，所以你可以只听不看。

Does EarMemo collect any data?

No. EarMemo does not collect any user data. There are no third-party SDKs, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. All data stays on your device and follows your own iCloud backup if you have iCloud enabled.

耳记会收集数据吗？

不会。耳记不收集任何用户数据。没有第三方 SDK、没有 analytics、没有广告、没有追踪。所有数据都只保存在你的设备上；如果你开启了 iCloud，会跟随你自己的 iCloud 备份。

Does EarMemo work on iPad?

Yes. EarMemo is a universal iOS app that runs on both iPhone and iPad, with a split-view layout adapted for the larger iPad screen.

耳记支持 iPad 吗？

支持。耳记是通用 iOS App，iPhone 和 iPad 都能用；iPad 上是为大屏适配的分栏布局。

Can EarMemo import files from other apps?

Yes. EarMemo integrates with the iOS Share Sheet, so you can send audio or video files into EarMemo from Messages, Mail, Files, or any app that exposes a share action for media files.

可以从其他 App 导入文件吗？

可以。耳记接入了 iOS 系统分享菜单——你可以从 信息 / 邮件 / 文件 或任何支持媒体文件分享的 App，把文件直接送进耳记。
