Headphones

Mark it without taking out your phone

Phone in your pocket, hands busy, and the line you want is going by. Press your AirPods twice: a short chime, and that second is saved — two seconds early, so it catches the start of the sentence. The words can come later.

  1. Set it once. Settings → Headphone Controls → Next: Bookmark Here
  2. Press twice on AirPods Pro (three times if you set Previous)
  3. Hear the chime. The mark is in your notes.
  • Every press is its own mark — unlimited, free version included
  • Car steering-wheel buttons follow the same setting

FAQ

What is EarMemo?

EarMemo is a local-first audio player for iOS, designed for long-form listening: podcasts, audiobooks, lectures, and voice recordings. All of your data — settings, playback progress, bookmarks, notes, and listening statistics — stays on your device. EarMemo has no server of its own, uploads nothing to the developer, and does not require an account.

Is EarMemo related to the AI study app with a similar name?

No. EarMemo is an offline audio player for iPhone and iPad, made by Fan YANG, for podcasts, audiobooks, lectures, and voice recordings you already own. It is a separate, unaffiliated product from any similarly named AI study, flashcard, or memorization service. EarMemo has no AI features, account, or app server.

How is EarMemo different from Apple Podcasts or Spotify?

EarMemo plays local audio files you already own — podcasts you've downloaded, audiobooks, lecture recordings, voice memos. It does not stream from a catalog, does not have a subscription, and does not require an account. It is designed for people who want one focused tool for long-form listening from files they control.

Does EarMemo work offline?

Yes. Playback, statistics, and notes work without an internet connection, including in airplane mode. Optional Wi-Fi file transfer needs Wi-Fi switched on and a local network.

Can I bookmark with my AirPods or other headphones?

Yes. In Settings → Headphone Controls, set Next or Previous to Bookmark Here. Pressing twice (Next) or three times (Previous) on AirPods Pro then marks the current second with a short chime, without taking out your phone. Other headphones use whichever button sends Next or Previous track, and car steering-wheel buttons follow the same setting. Marks are unlimited in the free version.

Which audio and video formats does EarMemo support?

EarMemo supports common audio formats including mp3, m4a, m4b (the standard audiobook container), aac, flac, and wav. Video files (mp4, mov, m4v) are played audio-only, so you can listen to the soundtrack of a video without watching it. DRM-protected purchases from Apple Books or Audible cannot be imported.

Does EarMemo support M4B chapters?

Yes. For M4B and other audiobooks with embedded chapters, EarMemo shows the table of contents, Previous and Next skip by chapter, and the progress bar shows the current chapter or the whole book — your choice — with the whole book's time left at the top. Notes are grouped by chapter, and the sleep timer can stop at the end of a chapter.

Does EarMemo collect any data?

No. EarMemo does not collect any user data. There are no third-party SDKs, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. All data stays on your device and follows your own iCloud backup if you have iCloud enabled.

Does EarMemo work on iPad?

Yes. EarMemo is a universal iOS app that runs on both iPhone and iPad, with a split-view layout adapted for the larger iPad screen.

Can EarMemo import files from other apps?

Yes. EarMemo integrates with the iOS Share Sheet, so you can send audio or video files into EarMemo from Messages, Mail, Files, or any app that exposes a share action for media files.

Is the free version a trial?

The free allowance never expires: listen to 1 album at a time; try 10 audio tracks total across album episodes and standalone tracks; add unlimited timestamp markers and write on 5 of them. Every import and playback tool remains available. Anything beyond the quota stays visible and deletable — it is only locked for playback until a one-time purchase of EarMemo Pro unlocks it.

I bought EarMemo when it was a paid download. Do I pay again?

No. Update to the latest version and EarMemo Pro unlocks automatically — no action needed, no second payment.
