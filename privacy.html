EarMemo

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-09

EarMemo does not collect, upload, or share any personal data, and it has no external server. Your data stays on your device; the only exception is when you explicitly enable Wi-Fi Transfer, which moves files directly between devices on your local network.

What stays on your device

Everything you do in EarMemo — imported audio, playback progress, listening stats, notes, bookmarks, and settings — stays on your device. If you have iCloud Backup enabled in iOS Settings, this data is included in your own iCloud backup as part of the standard system backup. EarMemo itself never initiates any sync or reads or writes any remote service.

What EarMemo does not do

  • Sends no requests to any external server.
  • Includes no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • Requests no access to your contacts, location, photos, microphone, or notifications.
  • Has no account system; no sign-in is required.

Wi-Fi Transfer

EarMemo offers optional Wi-Fi Transfer so you can import audio from a computer or another device. When you open it, EarMemo temporarily starts a local web service on your iPhone or iPad. A browser on the same Wi-Fi network connects directly to your device; files do not pass through an EarMemo server or cloud service. Closing the transfer screen stops the local service. When you are not using Wi-Fi Transfer, EarMemo makes no network connections.

System APIs used

EarMemo uses standard iOS APIs for settings, file access, background audio, Live Activities, and the optional local-network connection used by Wi-Fi Transfer. No third-party service or SDK receives your data.

Children's privacy

EarMemo is rated 4+ and is safe for children. The zero-collection policy applies to every user regardless of age.

Contact and policy updates

For questions, email earmemo@outlook.com. If this policy is ever updated, the new version ships with the next app update.
