# Pricing — EarMemo (耳记)

EarMemo is a paid iOS app with a single one-time purchase. There is no
subscription, no in-app purchase, no advertising, and no account.

## One-time purchase
- Price: US$4.99 (one-time; US App Store tier — local price varies by region)
- Billing: Pay once on the App Store. No recurring charges, ever.
- Includes: Every feature, plus all future updates to the app.
- Platforms: iPhone and iPad (universal app); also runs on Apple Silicon Macs.
- Requirements: iOS 17 / iPadOS 17 or later.
- Family Sharing: Supported (up to 6 family members).

## Not offered
- No subscription tier.
- No in-app purchases.
- No free trial — the price is a single US$4.99 purchase.
- No ads, no data collection, no account, no server.

## Where to buy
- App Store: https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6771007097
- Developer: Fan YANG

## Not to be confused with
EarMemo (耳记), the offline iOS audio player priced above, is a separate and
unaffiliated product from any similarly named AI study, flashcard, or
memorization service. This EarMemo has no AI features and never connects to a
server.
